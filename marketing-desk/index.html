How it works
1. Import your phone contacts in Contacts.
2. In Send, pick customers, pick the message language (English / Hinglish), pick a template and fill in the offer.
3. Press Start → for each customer press 💬 Open WhatsApp → press Send in WhatsApp → come back.
4. The tool remembers who got a message — they stay 🔒 locked for 15 days.
Keep your WhatsApp number safe
• Don't send more than 30–50 messages a day (limit in Settings).
• Only message people who know your shop.
• Use "Different message per customer" — sending the same text to everyone looks like spam to WhatsApp.
• If someone replies STOP, press "Said STOP" right away — they will never be messaged again.

How do I export contacts from my iPhone?
iPhone (iOS 16+): Open the Contacts app → tap Lists (top left) → long-press All Contacts → Export → Save to Files. Then tap "contacts file (.vcf)" above and choose that file.

Android: In Chrome you'll see a "📱 Pick from phone contacts" button to choose contacts directly. Or: Contacts app → Settings → Export → .vcf file.

Google Contacts: contacts.google.com → Export → Google CSV → upload it with the CSV button.
